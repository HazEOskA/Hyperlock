🔄 How the AI learns
Every 10 minutes the system checks if signals that got an EXECUTE verdict actually led to increased engagement.

If yes → it boosts the weight of whatever indicators fired.
If no → it reduces those weights.

Over time the AI gets smarter at separating real hype from fake noise.
WHAT IS HYPELOCK?
HYPELOCK monitors Farcaster, X, and Zora in real time, filters out bots and spam, and scores the genuine quality of crypto hype around any token.

When a signal is strong enough — it can automatically execute a trade or publish content to create more signal.

THE PIPELINE
01
Data Collection
Every 30 seconds the system fetches fresh posts from Farcaster, X, and trending mints from Zora — looking for tokens you're watching.
02
Alpha Filter v2 — Noise Removal
Every signal passes 5 filters before being scored:
🤖
Bot Detection
Unnatural like/comment ratios, round numbers, new accounts
🚫
Spam Keywords
"free airdrop", "100x gem", "guaranteed profit"...
📈
Velocity Spike
Engagement 5× above normal baseline in 60s window
🔁
Source Repetition
Same accounts engaging repeatedly = coordinated shilling
🎭
Coordination
5+ accounts acting in sync within 30 seconds
03
Adaptive Scoring
Clean signals are scored 0–100 across 6 indicators: likes, comments, shares, velocity, wallet quality, and engagement acceleration. The AI dynamically adjusts the weight of each indicator based on past performance.
04
Execution & Distribution
High-score signals trigger an action. In PAPER MODE this is a simulated trade. In LIVE MODE a real trade goes through Bankr. Either way, the signal is also published to X, Farcaster, and Zora to create new attention loops.
05
Self-Learning Loop
After 1h and 24h the system checks if its EXECUTE signals actually led to more engagement. It adjusts indicator weights accordingly — getting smarter with every cycle.
